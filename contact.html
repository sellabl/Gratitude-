Tell us about your foundation

Gratitude Giving Foundation is a nonprofit organization focused on strengthening foundations in areas related to health, gender equality, workforce development, reducing inequalities, and partnerships to achieve goals. Our mission is to serve as a connection between benefactors and foundations, positively contributing to their purposes.

The purpose of this survey is to generate proposals to contribute to your foundation and your mission. The more we know about you, the better we can create connections to enhance your reach and thus help strengthen your foundation.
