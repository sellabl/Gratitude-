FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What we do, who we help and how you can be part of our positive impact.

What does Gratitude Giving do?

Gratitude Giving is a foundation dedicated to connecting resources, benefactors, and foundations to drive meaningful projects that contribute to the well-being and development of communities internationally, operating virtually.

How can I contribute or volunteer?

You can contribute through donations, volunteer work, or support in events. Visit our “Get involved” section for more details on how to be part of our positive impact.

Who does the foundation benefit?

Our programs and services are focused on benefiting disadvantaged communities, concentrating on areas such as health, gender equality, workforce development, education, and reducing inequalities.

How are the donations used?

We are committed to using contributions efficiently and transparently, ensuring that every donation directly contributes to our charitable goals.

How can I stay informed about the foundation’s activities and events?

You can stay updated by following us on our social media (Facebook), and/or our blog with interesting resources (here). We regularly post about projects, achievements, and events we participate in.

How does the foundation select its charitable projects?

We select projects based on their alignment with our core pillars and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), prioritizing initiatives that have a sustainable positive impact.

Can I propose a project for the foundation to consider?

Yes, we encourage project proposals that align with our mission. If you have a proposal, write to us at contact@gratitudegivingfoundation.org for more information about the process.

What is the real impact of Gratitude Giving on the communities it supports?

We work to generate a measurable and positive impact on communities through specific projects. On our Current Projects page, we share testimonials and tangible results.

Still have questions?

We want you to be part of this great movement!

Write to us at contact@gratitudegivingfoundation.org or call us at 904 242 6889.
